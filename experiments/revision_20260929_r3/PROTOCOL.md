# Retrospective R3 comparison specification

This specification implements the reviewer-requested analysis after prior outcomes were inspected. It is not a prospective registration. No expected ranking, significance result or Panel B recovery is required.

Input: the original 480 cached streams in each of Panels D and E; original GLR-only additional stable summaries (160 per panel). Scoring and detector fitting are not repeated. Cached fitting source is own-checkpoint nominal replay. The first 40 windows give the median and 1.4826 MAD, with at least 32 eligible scores and scale at least 1e-12. Unsupported windows remain missing and hold the sequential state while advancing the clock. Both monitoring paths have 80 windows. GLR starts at monitoring; the exhaustive cut-search adapter uses all 120 windows, transforms eligible scores by Phi, and requires five eligible observations on each side of every cut. Its delta is 0.1. Its instantaneous path, not a fabricated monotone path, supplies strict first crossings.

For each statistic independently, take its 80-window stable maxima. Pooled LOCO takes the maximum of 152 foreign stable maxima. Arithmetic/geometric calibration divides each foreign maximum by its own checkpoint's eight-stream arithmetic/geometric mean; takes the maximum across 152 foreign values; and multiplies by the target's eight-stream divisor for changed and additional stable streams. Original stable evaluation excludes both modes of the target replicate, leaving six streams for its target divisor. The foreign divisor always uses eight. These shared/self-normalized foreign records do not establish an independent-sign or stream-rank guarantee.

For each statistic cross pooled, arithmetic and geometric calibration. Select the common multiplier from original stable records alone at per-mode caps D=(4,2), E=(4,1); floor-halved caps D=(2,1), E=(2,0); and (0,0). Changed outcomes never enter multiplier selection. The pooled cut-search family is a new LOCO family, distinct from the historical globally swept cut-search level. Historical global-level baseline results are regression-checked separately.

Separately regenerate arithmetic/geometric GLR frontiers at total caps 0,1,2,3,5,8 on the additional stable corpus. These are retrospectively tuned empirical frontiers; their per-mode alarm counts may differ. Native rules are also evaluated without retuning. The order-statistic ratio is a candidate; test actual `maximum > base_threshold * multiplier` and increment by `nextafter(...,+inf)` only if floating multiplication violates the cap. Report ties, candidate alarms, adjustment steps and actual counts. Do not claim every cap is attained. Miss delay is 81 and detected delay is the first monitoring window 1..80. Additional stable caches contain maxima only, so new stopping times for them are unavailable, not imputed.

For each statistic, arithmetic/geometric divisor, and six allocations of two replicate IDs versus their complement (both policy modes included), report the across-checkpoint SD change of mean log maxima, the 90th percentile of log maxima (linear interpolation), and maximum log maximum. Estimation and evaluation have four streams per checkpoint. Report all allocations as overlapping descriptive sensitivity analyses with no population interval. Also regenerate the existing eight-original/eight-additional GLR dispersion comparison. An eight-stream maximum is a diagnostic and differs from the maximum over 152 normalized foreign calibration records.

Export stream and window eligibility, maxima, divisor membership, foreign calibration assignment, thresholds, multipliers, all available stopping times, checkpoint results, and aggregate tables, with source/input/output hashes. Preserve D/E historical 50->68/80 and 0->4/80 endpoints and applicable baseline results. Panel B and new cut-search evaluation on the additional stable corpus remain excluded. No simulation is run.

## Senior-review corrections
A unit multiplier only means unscaled. Native held-out evaluation labels apply to the pre-existing frozen arithmetic GLR on additional streams. The geometric divisor was chosen after inspecting their null-dispersion results; its unscaled evaluation is retrospective reuse. New cut-search/calibration rules are also retrospective even at a unit multiplier. Multipliers remain the R2 ratio-order-statistic candidates advanced only to restore actual floating-point feasibility. No float-level minimality is claimed; two candidates have feasible adjacent predecessors, with no need to change the accepted operating points. R3 independently verifies the numerical construction and preserves all R2 scientific values.
